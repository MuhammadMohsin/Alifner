Privacy Policy

A clear note about what happens when you visit this website.

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by ALIFNER ("we," "us," or "our"). ALIFNER is a leather goods and jacket brand based in Pakistan.

We value your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information is handled when you visit our website.

For privacy questions, contact us through our official ALIFNER Etsy shop .

2. Information We Collect on This Website

This website does not provide account registration, contact forms, or on-site payment processing.

When you add products to your cart, the selected Etsy listing IDs may be saved in your browser's local storage so your cart can remain available while you browse.

This cart information is stored on your device and is not automatically sent to ALIFNER.

Your cart may be cleared when you follow the Etsy shopping link or when you clear your browser's local storage.

Our website hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver the website and help maintain security.

Hosting provider privacy practices and log retention are governed by the provider's own policies.

3. Etsy and External Links

Our website contains links to Etsy product listings and our Etsy shop.

When you visit Etsy, Etsy and its service providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Purchases, payments, messages, shipping details, order support, and other Etsy transactions are handled through Etsy.

ALIFNER does not receive your payment card details through this website.

Please review Etsy's Privacy Policy for details about how Etsy handles your information.

4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics

This website does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies.

The shopping cart uses browser local storage. Local storage is separate from cookies and helps keep selected listing IDs available during browsing.

This website may load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical connection information.

The cart contents are not included in Google Fonts requests.

5. How Information Is Used

Technical information processed by the website host may be used to deliver the website, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Information provided to Etsy is handled according to Etsy's policies and your interactions with Etsy and ALIFNER through that platform.

6. Data Retention and Security

This website does not maintain customer accounts or an order database.

Cart listing IDs remain in your browser until you leave for Etsy, remove them, or clear your browser storage.

Website hosting logs may be retained according to the hosting provider's settings and policies.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information.

For information associated with Etsy purchases, accounts, or messages, contact Etsy or reach ALIFNER through our Etsy shop.

For questions about information processed by our website host, contact us through the shop and identify the page or interaction involved.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children.

If a child has provided personal information through an external service, please contact the relevant service or reach ALIFNER through our Etsy shop.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or information practices change.

The updated date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please message ALIFNER through our official Etsy shop.
