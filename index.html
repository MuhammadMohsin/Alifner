01

Who we are

This website is operated by Alifner (“we,” “us,” or “our”), a leather goods and jacket brand based in Pakistan. For privacy questions, contact us through the official Alifner Etsy shop.

02

Information collected on this website

This website does not offer account registration, contact forms, or on-site payment. If you add a product to your cart, the product listing ID is saved in your browser’s local storage so the cart remains available after a reload. This cart information is stored on your device and is not sent to Alifner by this website. The cart is cleared when you follow an Etsy link from this site. You can also remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser details, and request time, to deliver and secure the site. The provider’s privacy terms and log retention settings apply to that processing.

03

Etsy and external links

Product and shop links take you to Etsy. If you follow a link, Etsy and its service providers may collect and use information under Etsy’s own privacy policy and terms. Purchases, payments, messages, shipping details, and order support are handled on Etsy. We do not receive your payment card details through this website. Review Etsy’s Privacy Policy for details.

04

Cookies, local storage, and analytics

This website does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. It uses browser local storage for the cart as described above. Local storage is separate from cookies.

The site loads typography from Google Fonts, which may receive technical connection information when the font files are requested. No font provider receives your cart contents through this site.

05

How information is used

Any technical information processed by the site’s host is used to deliver pages, maintain security, and troubleshoot service issues. Information you provide to Etsy is handled according to Etsy’s policies and your interactions with the shop.

06

Retention and security

We do not maintain customer accounts or an order database through this website. Cart listing IDs remain in your browser until you leave for Etsy, remove items, or clear this site’s browser storage.

Hosting request logs, if enabled, are retained according to the hosting provider’s settings. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

07

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. For information held by Etsy, contact Etsy or the shop through Etsy.

For information processed by this website’s host, contact us through the shop and identify the page or interaction involved.

08

Children’s privacy

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through an external service, contact us through the Etsy shop.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the site or its data practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.

10

Contact

For privacy questions, message Alifner through the official Etsy shop .
